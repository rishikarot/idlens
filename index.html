How action detection works
The app measures normalized eye-opening ratio (close → reopen), relative nose/eye shift, vertical nose/face ratio, mouth-corner/spread changes, and one-face continuity. Thresholds are heuristics calibrated to your neutral face, not measured degrees or a certified liveness score. The action order is randomized and every step expires after 12 seconds. Each camera session includes three guided retries, and each retry starts a fresh randomized sequence without stopping the video preview. A static photo normally fails, but sophisticated replay/virtual-camera attacks can still bypass this MVP.
